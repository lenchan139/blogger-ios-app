Before you write

Including the details below helps us resolve your issue faster:

  • App version — shown at the bottom of the Settings screen.
  • iOS version and device model — for example, iOS 26 on iPhone 17 Pro.
  • What you expected vs. what happened — a short description of the steps that led to the issue.
  • Screenshots or screen recording — if the problem is visual or hard to describe.

Common topics

  • Sign-in problems — make sure you grant both the Blogger and Google Drive (drive.file) scopes; you can review access at myaccount.google.com/permissions.
  • Image uploads — images are uploaded to your own Google Drive and referenced by a permanent URL. Check that Drive access was granted during sign-in.
  • Local drafts — unsaved edits live only on your device. Signing out or uninstalling the app removes them.
  • Data and privacy — see our Privacy Policy for what the app accesses and how it is handled.

Response times

This app is maintained by a small team, so replies may take a few business days. Email is the most reliable channel for anything that needs a written record.
